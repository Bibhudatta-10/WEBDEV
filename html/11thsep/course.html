Courses Offered

Lakshya Institute of Technology offers a range of undergraduate courses designed to provide students with knowledge, practical skills and a strong foundation for their future careers.

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)

BCA is an undergraduate program focused on computer applications and information technology. Students learn programming, software development, databases and other important concepts related to computer applications.

Bachelor of Information Technology and Management (ITM)

ITM combines information technology with management concepts. The course helps students understand both technical and management aspects of the modern IT industry.

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (B.Sc. CS)

B.Sc. Computer Science focuses on the fundamental concepts of computer science, programming and technology. It provides students with a foundation for further studies and careers in the technology field.

Bachelor of Science in Data Science (B.Sc. DS)

Data Science focuses on working with data and using computational and analytical techniques to understand information. Students develop knowledge that can be useful in areas such as data analysis, programming and modern data-driven technologies.

Choose Your Path

Every course offers a different learning experience and career direction. Students can choose a program according to their interests, strengths and future goals.
